# Melody, Counterpoint & Polyphonic Listening Lab

Interactive companion to Chapter 6 of *Musical Representations and Models: Perception, Structure, and Computation*.

## Purpose

The lab presents the five species of counterpoint as controlled listening and generation experiments:

1. **First Species — Align**
2. **Second Species — Connect**
3. **Third Species — Shape**
4. **Fourth Species — Delay & Syncopation**
5. **Fifth Species — Integrate**

Students can hear the cantus firmus and counterpoint separately or together, inspect pitch trajectories and rule audits, generate alternative valid counterpoints, and—in Fifth Species—compare hard-rule generation with optional phrase-level heuristic ranking.

## Running locally

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser. No installation or server is required.

## Pedagogical note

The numerical and heuristic displays are transparent teaching aids. They are not intended as universal measures of musical quality or beauty.
