Melody, Counterpoint & Polyphonic Listening

Explore how independent melodic lines interact with vertical sonority through the five species of counterpoint. Listen to each line alone or together, inspect the rule audit, generate alternative valid realizations, and compare strict rule-based generation with optional phrase-level heuristics.

How to use this lab

The five species are presented as controlled musical experiments rather than as a list of prohibitions. Each stage isolates a different relationship between horizontal melodic motion and vertical consonance.

Suggested workflowChoose a species, listen to the cantus firmus and counterpoint separately, then hear them together. Inspect the trajectory and rule audit, and use Generate another valid counterpoint to compare different legal solutions. In Fifth Species, switch the heuristic layer on and off to distinguish admissibility from aesthetic preference.

The numerical and heuristic displays are pedagogical descriptors. They do not claim to measure musical beauty or replace musical judgement.
